Pick something on the map

Pan by dragging, zoom with the wheel or the buttons. Nothing on the map is labeled until you answer it. Every building, street and landmark gets one answer.

Test area: the walkable campus inside Cherry Ln, McCormick Rd, US 231, Northwestern Ave and Grant St, plus the key monuments and landmarks around it. Items marked ★ after you answer are required to pass: miss one and you retest.

What is this called?

Copy the spelling from the Word bank so a typo doesn’t cost you the point. Capitalization doesn’t matter. For streets, “St” and “Street” are the same, and the direction (N, S, E, W) is optional.
